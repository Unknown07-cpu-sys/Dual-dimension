Dual Dimensions - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: Dual Dimensions does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game stores

Your progress (unlocked levels, best times) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your own device, using the browser/app's local storage. This data never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.

What we do not do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs. The game does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files. It works fully offline.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Unknown07-cpu-sys/Dual-dimension/issues.
